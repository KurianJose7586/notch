Privacy policy

The app

  • I collect nothing. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no crash reporting and no ads, and I receive nothing from you.
  • Update checks. Every few hours the app asks GitHub whether a newer release exists, and downloads it if so. GitHub sees your IP address and the app version, as with any download, under GitHub's privacy statement. You can turn this off in Setup. Nothing else leaves your computer.
  • Agent hooks stay local. Agents report to the app over http://127.0.0.1:47800, a loopback address that other machines can't reach.
  • Phone approval (off until you turn it on). If you enable it, the app serves a page on your local Wi-Fi so you can allow or deny requests from your phone. Anyone on that network who has the pairing link can use it, so keep the link private; "New link" in Setup replaces it. It never goes over the internet.
  • What it reads. Events from your agents: project name, session, task, tool being used, permission requests, progress, and (for Claude Code, via its status line) usage-limit percentages. It also reads your agents' config files to add or remove its hooks.
  • What it stores. Small files in the app's own data folder on your PC: recently used projects, session names you chose, and a flag that setup finished. Uninstalling removes the app; delete its data folder to remove these.
  • Nothing is shared by me with anyone, because I collect nothing.

Your agents themselves (Claude Code, Antigravity, etc.) send data to their own providers under their own policies. Agent Notch doesn't change that and isn't part of it.

This website

  • It is a static page with no cookies, no analytics and no forms.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address and browser details when the page loads, under Google's privacy policy.
  • Links to GitHub take you to a site with its own policy. Downloads are served by GitHub.
  • If you open an issue or contact me on GitHub, that is handled under GitHub's terms and I see what you choose to post.

Your rights

Since I hold no personal data about you, there's nothing to access, correct or delete on my side. Questions or concerns? Open an issue.
